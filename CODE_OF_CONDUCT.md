# Code of conduct

Bilge's community is open to everyone who wants to use, improve or discuss it, whatever their background, language
or experience.

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent, and remember many people here write in their second language.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people. Harassment, insults and discriminatory language are not accepted.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and block those who keep
breaking them. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[vrnsoft.com.tr](https://vrnsoft.com.tr).

**Türkçe:** Herkese saygılı ve sabırlı ol; fikirleri ve kodu eleştir, insanları değil. Taciz, hakaret ve ayrımcı
dil kabul edilmez.
